# Reviewed period coverage

The existing contract and default behavior remain unchanged. Set
`ASTROFRIEND_EXTENDED_PERIOD_RULES=1` only after the gateway and consuming
backend accept `reviewed-outcomes-v3`. Contract 1 remains unchanged even with
the flag enabled. `VEDASTRO_NATIVE_FINANCE_ENABLED=1` additionally requires
the self-hosted API's `ReadingEvidence` operation to support topic `finance`.
Both flags default to zero.

The small period manifest pins the official stable engine revision and source
hash. It contains four category ratings for all 81 PD1/PD2 combinations and
eight explicitly reviewed personal-marriage indications. Household weddings,
marriage expenses and engagement-only entries are excluded. Copies used by
Python and the packaged gateway must be byte-identical.

Extended marriage windows are calculated Vimshottari intervals associated with
those selected traditional source entries. They are conditional candidates;
they do not establish an exact wedding year, event probability or a complete
transit/divisional timing analysis. Adult and birth-uncertainty guards apply.
The declared priority favors a Jupiter subperiod with natal Jupiter in house
seven; it is not a calibrated score. The scan looks eight years ahead for
candidate starts and returns at most two windows.

Existing reviewed Venus meanings are retained. Other current periods use
family/relationship, study or money category meanings for their respective
topics. Money categories alone do not establish job or promotion outcomes.
Career readings retain separately reviewed career-house interpretations.
Native strength, aspects and divisions remain separate calculation facts;
they are not used to manufacture event dates or partner characteristics.

The native finance adapter uses the second-house ruler and preserves Lahiri,
true-node and 365.25-day conventions. Its native bhava-based strength remains
separate from whole-sign placement interpretation. Geometry, D9 and strength
components are checked by the same provider guard as other topics.

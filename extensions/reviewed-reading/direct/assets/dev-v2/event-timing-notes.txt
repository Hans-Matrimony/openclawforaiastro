Pilot event-timing assessment, version 1

Enable only with ASTROFRIEND_PILOT_EVENT_TIMING_ENABLED=1. The caller also
requires the existing exact-account WhatsApp coherent-reading pilot gates.
The feature is off by default. The legacy chart engine and other accounts,
surfaces, matching, horary and explicit planetary schedules are unchanged.

What is calculated

- Use the successful selected natal chart, its verified birth timestamp and
  precise Swiss Ephemeris Moon longitude. Use the same Lahiri/Moshier basis.
- Calculate full Vimshottari major/sub-period boundaries using 365.25-day
  years. The first full period starts before birth when part has elapsed;
  do not subdivide only the shortened remaining balance.
- Scan up to 60 months at daily noon-UTC samples. Intersect topic-linked
  sub-periods with calculated future Jupiter occupation/aspect of the
  primary topic houses. Retain Saturn transit pressures as contrary evidence.
- Marriage uses primary house 7; relationship prospects use 5/7; career uses
  10; money uses 2/11. Secondary house ownership/occupation, supportive aspects
  of primary houses and significators establish period relevance, not success
  probabilities. Current and future period links use these same rules in the
  presentation packet; the legacy ownership/occupation-only period summary is
  excluded when this richer timing evidence is available. Other natal factors
  and the legacy chart calculation remain unchanged.
- A pressure-only minor-period aspect qualifies only with a relevant,
  non-adverse major lord AND the independent future Jupiter activation.
- Keep continuous intervals of at least 28 days, and return at most the
  earliest three. These are selected opportunities, not an exhaustive ranking.
  The reply receives the earliest interval at MONTH precision only.

Interpretation and limits

This conjunction of rules is an experimental product heuristic inspired by
traditional astrology. It is not an empirically calibrated event predictor or
an exact reproduction of a cited author's complete interpretation system.
Correct ephemeris calculations do not validate the predicted life event.
The first reply bubble may describe the month range as potentially supportive;
it must not claim a guaranteed wedding, job, financial outcome or exact day.
Explain the period/transit basis afterward, retaining adverse natal factors.

No qualifying interval is a valid result; do not substitute a dasha boundary.
A particular person's feelings, consent, decision or return is outside this
assessment. General relationship windows must not be presented as that
person's return dates. Health, death, education and unsupported topics do not
receive event dates from this module. Marriage windows before age 18 are
excluded. A request for a precise day cannot increase month-level precision.

The legacy bundled city geocoder has fixed offsets, including in DST cities.
Version 1 therefore accepts only its bundled Indian-city +05:30 birth basis;
other historical timezones fail closed. Conflicting natal/period evidence also
fails closed rather than mixing two calendars. The new calendar preserves
the full first-period anchor, but does not silently replace a conflicting
legacy natal assessment; that requires a separately verified migration.

# Tarot Guardrails

This file documents the intended safety behavior for the Tarot reader.

- Tarot is reflective and symbolic, not deterministic.
- Never predict death, disease, pregnancy, legal outcomes, or guaranteed money.
- Never pressure the user into decisions.
- Never claim another person's hidden thoughts as fact.
- Never replace medical, legal, financial, or emergency advice.
- Keep language respectful and emotionally safe.
- Keep the Meera/Aarav astrologer flow separate.
